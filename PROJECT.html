SERVED EVERYDAY SINCE 1990


Located in the heart of North India, Silver Spoon is an award-winning restaurant driven by a passion for love, an ingredient at the heart of Indian cooking. Chef Fermin and his team curate a seasonally driven menu with an emphasis on bringing together traditional Indian cooking techniques with local ingredients to create a unique and exciting dining experience. Our bar offers some of India's most unique agave and succulent spirits state-side, as well as highlighting a rotating selection of natural drinks and craft cocktails. Our Palace is located near Axis Bank, Malviya Nagar, Jaipur, the dining room and bar occupy a series of rooms with floor to 16-foot-ceiling windows leading to an open kitchen.
